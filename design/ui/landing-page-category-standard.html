People, employees, and runtimes have distinct roles.

Avrig models a company around human authority. An AI employee has an identity that persists beyond any one execution. The runtime that performs a task is temporary.

Founder
Human owner with authority over significant organizational decisions and approved baselines.
CEO / PET
AI Employee #001, responsible for company operations, projects, departments, and workforce coordination.
Personal Assistant
A separate AI position serving the Founder on personal and administrative tasks.
Employees
Persistent human or AI organizational identities, with roles, department membership, history, and project assignments.
Actor
The shared participant identity used by a human or AI employee in tasks, meetings, approvals, and audits.
Agent runtime
An ephemeral execution process. It may start, pause, sleep, or terminate without deleting the employee identity.

Two dimensions stay separate: departments describe organizational home; project teams describe execution context. The virtual office is a projection of structured records, not the source of truth.

Agents can prepare the work. People approve it.

An agent may analyze, recommend, draft, and execute permitted actions. An agent cannot approve an artifact. Human approval creates the authority to make a candidate an approved baseline.

Every claim and artifact carries its standing.

Authority, proposal, practice, and uncertainty are named explicitly. Traceability connects specific versions, so an evidence link records what it actually refers to.

Epistemic status

Knowledge does not become authoritative by appearing in an agent response. These six states distinguish project truth, practice, advice, inference, and uncertainty.

  • AuthoritativeRequirementAuthorized project constraint, authoritative
  • ApprovedBaselineFormally approved project artifact, authoritative
  • ExistingPracticeObserved current practice, not automatically authoritative
  • BestPracticeRecommended platform guidance
  • AgentInferenceAI-generated proposal or hypothesis
  • AssumptionOrUnknownUnverified item that needs clarification
Precedence rule: project requirements and approved baselines take precedence over platform guidance when they conflict.

Knowledge follows the work and keeps its source.

Project knowledge captures the truth and constraints for a specific effort. Platform knowledge stores reusable guidance. Retrieval stays scoped to the active lifecycle stage, and conflicts are surfaced for human deliberation.

  1. StoreKeep source documents, metadata, and artifacts in authoritative records.
  2. IngestParse and normalize material while retaining provenance.
  3. IndexCombine semantic and lexical search over structured content.
  4. ResolveRetrieve stage-scoped context and apply authority precedence.
  5. TraceLink sources, decisions, and outputs; detect discrepancies.
  6. PromoteReview reusable lessons before adding them to platform guidance.

The technology architecture follows the same boundaries.

Clients, services, governed execution, knowledge processing, and persistence are defined as connected layers. The architecture puts human authority and traceable records across the system.

Target technology definition, not implementation status

Client surfaces

Web application with React and Tailwind CSS, a Tauri desktop shell, and a Capacitor mobile shell.

Service core

Rust services with Axum and Tokio provide API routing and event delivery.

Identity and policy

OIDC identity through Rauthy and explicit policy enforcement through Cedar.

Governed execution

Typed tool gateways and sandbox boundaries separate agents from direct host access.

Knowledge processing

Document parsing, semantic and lexical search, stage-scoped retrieval, precedence resolution, and reranking.

Authoritative persistence

PostgreSQL for relational records and search, Apache AGE for graph relationships, and Garage for S3-compatible artifacts.

The Technology Architecture is a target design. The repository contains both implemented modules and empty placeholders; this page does not imply that every architecture component is shipped.
