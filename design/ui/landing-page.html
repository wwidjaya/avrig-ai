THE WORKFLOW

Progress you can inspect

Each stage has a defined purpose, a responsible lead, and a record of the work it produces. Move forward when the evidence is ready.

01 / DEFINE

Set the intent

Capture the project outcome and the requirements it must satisfy.

02 / DESIGN

Establish the baseline

Shape the data, architecture, and platform decisions into reviewable artifacts.

03 / BUILD

Carry out the work

Teams execute the planned stages and keep their artifacts versioned.

04 / VERIFY

Review the evidence

Check results and approve a specific candidate version before it advances.

BUILT AROUND ACCOUNTABILITY

Clear roles. Checkable work.

Avrig makes the boundary between agent execution and human authority part of the workflow. Every claim can be followed back to a source and version.

01

Human approval stays human

Agents can prepare and execute permitted work. A human authority reviews and ratifies stage baselines.

02

Evidence keeps its context

Artifacts retain version pins, provenance, and their relationships to requirements and verification.

03

Stages keep their order

The lifecycle makes prerequisites and review points visible as work progresses.

04

State is explicit

Requirements, approved baselines, existing practice, and agent-generated claims carry distinct status.
