HUMAN DECISION & APPROVAL

Agents recommend.
People decide.

Autonomy governs what an agent can do. Authority governs what it may decide. Avrig keeps those responsibilities distinct and records material decisions for review.

01 / PROPOSE

Agents make the case

Recommendations can include rationale, alternatives, evidence, assumptions, constraints, risks, and uncertainty.

02 / REVIEW

People see the context

The human can inspect the recommendation, related requirements, project knowledge, source evidence, and affected artifacts.

03 / DECIDE

Several decisions are possible

Adopt, modify, reject, defer, or request revision. A recommendation is not approval by itself.

04 / RECORD

The outcome stays traceable

Material decisions retain who decided, why, when, and which recommendation or artifact version was considered.
