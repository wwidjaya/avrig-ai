HUMAN DECISION & APPROVAL

Agents recommend.
People decide.

Greater agent autonomy does not imply greater authority. Avrig separates analysis and execution from human decisions and formal artifact approval.

01Agent recommendation

Review rationale, alternatives, sources, assumptions, risks, and uncertainty. Agent-generated content is non-authoritative by default.

02Human review

Inspect the recommendation alongside applicable requirements, project knowledge, supporting evidence, and affected artifacts.

03Human decision

Adopt, modify, reject, defer, or request revision. The recorded decision explains what the human chose.

04Controlled outcome

Where required, formal approval establishes an artifact baseline with version, provenance, and traceable relationships.

Agent proposes→Human reviews→Human decides→Outcome recorded
