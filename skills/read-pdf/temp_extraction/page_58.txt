**SEE chart, pie.**: citizen A person recognized as a member of a public state, with associated obligations and rights. Not the same as customer. citizen advocacy The perception that a government does what is best for its citizens, not just what is best for its own bottom line. Citizen Data Integration (CDI) Solutions for capturing and maintaining accurate, up-to-date data about individual citizens and delivering information in an actionable form “just in time” at citizen touch points. A specialized form of Master Data Management, focusing on citizen master data. Citizen Relationship Management (CRM) Establishing relationships with individual citizens and then using that information to treat different citizens differently. Census profiles and taxpayer analysis are examples of decision support activities that can affect the success of citizen relationships. Effective CRM is dependent on high quality master data about individuals and organizations (citizen data integration). clarity A measurement that evaluates freedom from obscurity or extraneous data. class
