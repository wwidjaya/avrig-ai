**DAMA International**: An international not-for-profit association of data resource management professionals with chapters and members-at-large around the world, dedicated to advancing the concepts and practices of managing data, information and knowledge as enterprise assets. DAMA International is the leading data management professional organization worldwide. DAMA-DMBOK Functional Framework The organizing structure for the DAMA-DMBOK Guide, consisting of a functional decomposition of ten data management functions mapped against seven environmental elements (Goals & Principles, Activities, Deliverables, Roles & Responsibilities, Practices & Techniques, Technology, and Organization & Culture). (DAMA-DMBOK GUIDE 1st edition, pg. 12)
