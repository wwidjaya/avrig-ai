1. The definition states that snapshot --app does not raise AMBIGUOUS_TARGET when an application has several windows, and silently takes the focused window. Measured against agent-desktop 1.3.1 on this host: when no window of that application holds focus it DOES raise AMBIGUOUS_TARGET with candidate_count 2 and both window ids (envelopes/E3-focused-a.json). The silent pick happens only when one of the app's windows is already focused, which is the rc.001 observation. The remedy the definition gives (pin --window-id whenever list-windows shows more than one window) is correct and remains correct.
