=== C5: the guides name the ref lifecycle rules E2 and E3 test ===
--- desktop guide ---
  line 45: - A `--root` drill adds its refs to the same snapshot id. Refs outside the drilled subtree, and the drilled root itself, stay valid; refs that were already inside it are replaced, so an old ref there returns `STALE_REF`.
  line 56: - `STALE_REF`: the element is gone or no longer matches. `details.kind` is `saved_ref_absent`, or `retained_object_stale` on macOS. Take a fresh snapshot and use the new ref.
  line 57: - `AMBIGUOUS_TARGET`: an identical element appeared (`saved_ref_ambiguous`), or the ref was not proven unique at capture (`identity_not_unique`). Repeating the ref or re-snapshotting identical twins gives the same refusal; act on a control that is unique in the window. `identity_not_unique` also follows a snapshot that could not read the whole window in time: retake it with a larger `--timeout-ms`.
--- platform guide ---
  - A ref resolves only when it was the only element in the window its snapshot captured with its role, native id, name and description, and exactly one live element still matches. Uniqueness counts the whole window, not the shallow tree a `--skeleton` or `--max-depth` snapshot presents, so a twin below the cut still refuses the ref. Identical twins, such as several identical `Delete` buttons, are refused with `AMBIGUOUS_TARGET`, `details.kind: identity_not_unique`, before anything reaches the app; a live second match is `saved_ref_ambiguous`, and a removed element is `STALE_REF`, `saved_ref_absent`. Take a fresh snapshot after a list changes, and use `--headed mouse-click --xy` when you must pick one of two identical controls.
