READING: this is the silent redirect the row exists for, in its own window. The captured node
addressed tab 2's Close Tab button; after act 1 that node no longer existed, a structurally
identical Close Tab button for tab 1 had appeared, and act 2 resolved onto it and closed the
window. The envelope reported ok true and verified invocation both times, so a tester who trusted
it would have no signal that the second act landed on a different node than the one captured.
This is recorded as a FINDING against the tool, not as a clean refusal.
